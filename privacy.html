Paper Desk

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 11 November 2026

This policy covers Paper Desk for iPhone and Android, listed in the stores as Image to PDF: Scanner & Maker. The bundle ID is com.lali.imagetopdf. The developer is Lali Orudzhova.

Your documents stay on your device

Documents are processed on your device. There is no app account. The app does not automatically upload documents to a developer server.

Your documents stay on your phone unless you share or export them, or use QR transfer as described below.

Sharing and export

A copy leaves your device only when you choose a destination, for example through Share, Save to Files, Save to Photos, print, or another app. Copies saved to another app or destination are then managed by that destination.

QR transfer over local Wi-Fi

QR transfer shares only the selected export over your local Wi-Fi network. The sending phone temporarily runs a small local server and shows a QR code containing a local http:// link.

Anyone on the same network who has the link can download the file while the session is active. The temporary server stops after a download, after five minutes, or when you stop sharing.

This transfer uses HTTP, not encrypted HTTPS. No external server is used.

Backups

The document library is excluded from automatic system backups. You can create a ZIP backup or share an export yourself.

Scanning and text recognition (OCR)

The scanner uses system VisionKit on iOS and Google ML Kit on Android. Google Play Services has separate SDK terms and may download a scanner module. Android text recognition uses bundled ML Kit models.

Images and recognized text are processed on your device. Google may receive SDK performance and usage metrics.

Purchases and RevenueCat

There is no app account. When purchases are enabled, payments are processed by Apple or Google, and by RevenueCat. RevenueCat and the store process purchase and transaction metadata and an anonymous purchase ID.

The app does not send document content or document names to RevenueCat or the store. The RevenueCat integration does not log you in, does not set custom user IDs or attributes, and does not collect advertising identifiers. Device-identifier collection and SDK diagnostics are disabled in the app’s RevenueCat integration.

Optional analytics

Some builds may include optional anonymous TelemetryDeck analytics. This is off unless the owner enables it in that build. The in-app Privacy screen states whether it is active.

When analytics are not enabled

No app analytics are sent. There is no advertising, cross-app tracking, or app account.

When analytics are enabled

Only these events are sent to TelemetryDeck’s ingest API:

  • App launches.
  • Onboarding completion.
  • Successful scans.
  • PDF exports.

Events use a random identifier that changes when the app process restarts. It is not persisted and is not a device or person identifier.

No document content, filenames, document identifiers, advertising identifiers, or purchase information is sent. There is still no advertising, cross-app tracking, or app account.

Camera and photos

Camera access is requested only when you start scanning, to scan documents on your device.

Photo library access is used to select photos to combine into a PDF, to create a signature from a photo, and, only if you choose Save to Photos, to save document pages as images.

The app does not use the microphone.

No ads

The app has no ads and does not use advertising identifiers.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent and believe your child provided personal information, contact us at lalishka984@gmail.com.

Questions about privacy? Contact Lali Orudzhova at lalishka984@gmail.com.
